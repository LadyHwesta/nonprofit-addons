Staff & Volunteer Guide

Nonprofit & Volunteer Handbook

How to run the day-to-day work in the system — recording gifts, keeping funds straight, producing board and IRS reports, and scheduling volunteers.

Written for people who don't think of themselves as “the software person.” Work through Part 1 once when you set up. After that, jump to whatever you're doing today. Anything in a grey pill is a menu path — click each word in turn along the top or left of the screen.

Part 1

One-time setup

Do this once, in order, before the first real transaction. Mostly the treasurer's job. It assumes a U.S. chart of accounts is already loaded and you can sign in as an administrator.

Turn on analytic accounting

  1. Go to Settings › Accounting and switch on Analytic Accounting. This is what lets you tag a transaction with a fund. Everything else here depends on it.

Add the nonprofit chart of accounts

  1. Open Accounting › Configuration › Add Non-Profit Accounts.

  2. Leave Code Prefix blank unless the new account numbers (4000–5900 range) collide with numbers your localization already uses. If they do, enter something like 9 so the accounts land at 94000, 95000, and so on.

  3. Keep Skip Codes That Already Exist ticked and click Add Accounts. It adds contribution, grant, special-events, in-kind, functional-expense, and net-asset accounts on top of your existing chart — it never changes or removes anything.

Safe to re-run

If you're not sure whether it ran, run it again. Accounts that already exist are skipped, so a second run adds nothing.

Set the fund-accounting options

  1. Go to Settings › Accounting › Fund Accounting (Non-Profit).

  2. Under Net Assets Released accounts, click Detect from chart. It fills the two clearing accounts the restriction-release entry uses.

  3. Pick a Default Program and Default Function — these pre-fill on a release so you're not choosing them every time.

  4. Leave Require a Fund on income & expense off for now.

Do this last

Enter your opening balances and any historical entries first. Only then turn on Require a Fund on income & expense. Once it's on, the system won't let you post an income or expense line that isn't 100% assigned to a fund — which is exactly what you want going forward, but it would block your opening entries.

Set the donation options

  1. In Settings › Accounting, find the Donation section and set the bank account donations land in and the product used for bank-transfer gifts.

Set the volunteer options

  1. Go to Settings › Events.

  2. Set the volunteer reminder lead time (days before an event that confirmed volunteers get an email).

  3. Under Volunteer shifts: default shift length, the check-in grace window (how many minutes before a shift starts a volunteer may check in from their phone), and the recurring-shift horizon (how many weeks ahead the nightly job builds repeating shifts).

  4. Under Volunteer screening: choose the Volunteer Coordinator (they get the reminder when a background check is about to lapse), and how long a background check and a signed waiver stay valid.

Part 2

Funds, programs & functions

Three questions get asked of most transactions. Answering them consistently is the whole game — the reports build themselves from these answers.

Fund — whose money is it?

Every dollar belongs to a net-asset class. The starter funds are:

  • Unrestricted — the donor put no strings on it. Most gifts.
  • With Donor Restrictions – Purpose — must be spent on a specific program or thing.
  • With Donor Restrictions – Time — can't be spent until a future period.
  • Perpetual (Endowment) — the principal is never spent.

Program — which mission activity?

Your actual programs: a food pantry, an after-school club, a clinic day. Add one Program per activity. Grant and board reporting slice by this.

Function — what kind of work?

The three Form 990 columns: Program Services, Management & General, Fundraising. Every expense is one of these.

Grant — which award?

Add one Grant per award you receive, so you can report drawdowns and spending against that funder.

Where you enter it

On an invoice, bill, or journal-entry line there's an Analytic Distribution field. Click it and set the fund to 100% (split across two funds if a gift is genuinely shared). Add the Program and Function the same way. That one field feeds every report in Part 5.

Part 3

Recording money

The bookkeeper's weekly work. The pattern is always the same: record the transaction, then set the fund (and Program / Function) on the income or expense line.

A cash or check donation

  1. Open Donations › Donations › New.

  2. Choose the donor, the date, and the payment method. Add a line for what was given — the contribution account is picked for you.

  3. On that line, set Analytic Distribution: Unrestricted fund for an ordinary gift.

  4. Validate. The system posts the journal entry and can produce a tax receipt for the donor.

No donation module? Record it as a bank entry or customer invoice against a Contributions account instead — the donor reports and year-end statements still pick it up as long as the account carries its contribution tag.

A restricted gift

  1. Record it the same way, but choose Contributions – With Donor Restrictions as the account and set the fund to With Donor Restrictions – Purpose (or – Time).

  2. Note the restriction — what it's for, or when it frees up — on the record so whoever releases it later knows the terms.

An in-kind gift (goods or services)

  1. Record a journal entry: debit the asset or expense it represents, credit In-Kind Contributions, at fair value.

  2. Set the fund on both lines. Keep the donor's description and how you valued it with the entry.

A vendor bill

  1. Enter the bill as normal under Accounting › Vendors › Bills.

  2. On each expense line set Analytic Distribution: the Fund it's paid from, the Program it serves, and the Function (Program Services / Management & General / Fundraising). Split a line if it's shared — e.g. 70% Program, 30% Management & General.

A fundraising event

Put ticket and sponsorship income to Fundraising Events – Gross Revenue. Put the cost of what donors received in return (the dinner, the band) to Fundraising Events – Direct Benefit to Donor. The Statement of Activities nets them, which is what the 990 expects.

Part 4

Releasing restricted funds

When a donor's purpose has been met or a time restriction has passed, you move that money from the restricted class to unrestricted. You don't hand-write the journal entry — the release wizard does.

  1. Open Accounting › Non-Profit › Restriction Releases › New.

  2. Released From: the restricted fund. Released To: normally Unrestricted.

  3. Enter the amount now free, the date, and optionally the Program and Function it relates to.

  4. Add a note — which grant, which board vote — then click Post.

Posting creates one balanced journal entry between the two Net Assets Released accounts, carrying both funds. On the Statement of Activities it shows as net assets released from restrictions: a reduction in the restricted column, an equal increase in the unrestricted column, zero to the bottom line.

Made a mistake?

Open the release and click Cancel. It reverses the journal entry for you. Don't delete the entry by hand.

Checking your work

Accounting › Non-Profit › Net-Asset Roll-Forward shows, per fund: opening balance, revenue, expense, releases in and out, closing balance — for any date range.

Part 5

Board & IRS reporting

Because every transaction carries its fund, program, and function, these come out without extra bookkeeping.

For the board

  • Statement of Activities — revenue by source, expense by function, and the change in net assets. Find it under Accounting › Reporting › MIS Reports; open the Current Year instance.
  • Statement of Financial Position — assets, liabilities, and net assets split into with and without donor restrictions.

To show these by fund as separate columns, add fund-filtered periods to the report instance.

For the Form 990 preparer

  • Functional Expenses (990 Part IX) — Accounting › Reporting › Non-Profit › Functional Expenses. A grid of every natural expense account against Program / Management & General / Fundraising.

For fundraising

  • Donor Analytics — Accounting › Reporting › Non-Profit › Donor Analytics. One row per donor: lifetime giving, this year, last year, first / last / largest gift. Use the LYBUNT filter (gave Last Year But Unfortunately Not This) to build a lapsed-donor appeal, and SYBUNT (Some Year…) for a deeper reactivation list.
  • Year-End Contribution Statements — Accounting › Reporting › Non-Profit › Year-End Contribution Statements. Choose the year and a minimum total (defaults to $250), leave the donor list blank for everyone, and print. Each donor gets one PDF listing every gift and the total, with the IRS written-acknowledgement and quid-pro-quo language.

Part 6

Volunteer roles & shifts

The coordinator's setup for each event. A role can be filled as one block for the whole event, or broken into timed shifts.

Add roles to an event

  1. Open the event, go to its Volunteers tab.

  2. Add a role — a name (“Setup Crew”, “Greeter”, “Net Control”), how many people it needs, and a short description volunteers will see on the website.

Run a role as shifts

  1. On the role, tick Run as Shifts.

  2. Add shifts — each is a time window and a slot count. Or use the Shifts stat button on the event to see them on a calendar.

Recurring shifts (a weekly program)

  1. On an Event Type (Events › Configuration › Event Types), add Recurring Volunteer Shift lines: role name, weekday, start time, length, slots.

  2. Give your recurring event that type. A nightly job creates the matching shifts for as far ahead as the horizon in settings, and won't duplicate ones it already made.

  3. Need them sooner? Open the event and click Generate shifts from event type.

On the day

  • Front desk opens the shift and clicks Check In as each volunteer arrives, Check Out when they leave. Hours are calculated automatically.
  • Someone didn't show? No-show on their assignment — it's kept for the record but left out of hours reports.

Shift swaps

A volunteer can offer a shift for swap from their own page. Another volunteer claims it. The coordinator approves it under Events › Shift Swaps, which reassigns the shift.

Reporting

  • Volunteer Hours (Events › Volunteer Reporting › Volunteer Hours) — a pivot by volunteer, program, event, or month.
  • Hours Certificates — a per-volunteer PDF for a chosen year, listing every shift and the total. Good for students and court-ordered service.
  • Understaffed Shifts — upcoming shifts still short of people.

Part 7

Screening volunteers

For roles that need a cleared, trained, or specifically-skilled person. Set up on the volunteer's contact record, under the Volunteer tab.

Skills

Tag volunteers with skills (First Aid, Spanish, Licensed Driver…). Manage the list under Events › Configuration › Volunteer Skills.

Background checks

  1. When you start a check, set the state to Requested.

  2. When it clears, click Mark Cleared — it stamps today's date and sets the re-check due date from the validity in settings.

  3. A nightly job flips lapsed checks to Expired and, ahead of expiry, raises a to-do for the Volunteer Coordinator. You don't have to watch the calendar.

Liability waiver

  • Print the waiver from the contact's Print menu (it adapts for minors). Collect the signed copy.
  • Click Record Waiver Accepted to stamp the date. It counts as current for the validity period in settings.

Minors & emergency contact

Tick Under 18 and record the parent or guardian; add an emergency contact name and phone. The waiver PDF picks up the guardian automatically.

Make a role require it

  1. On the volunteer role, tick Requires Background Check and/or Requires Waiver, and add any Required Skills.

  2. Now a sign-up — on the website or in the back office — from a volunteer who isn't cleared, hasn't a current waiver, or lacks a skill is refused, with a message saying exactly what's missing.

Finding the right people

On a shift, click Suggest Volunteers. You get the people who have every required skill, are cleared and waivered as the role needs, and aren't already booked on an overlapping shift.

Part 8

The volunteer's own view

What a volunteer sees when they sign in. Worth walking a new volunteer through once.

  1. Sign in and open My Volunteer Shifts from the account menu, or go straight to /my/shifts.

  2. Upcoming and Past shifts are listed, with a running total of lifetime hours.

  3. When a shift is about to start (within the grace window) a Check in button appears; a Check out button appears once checked in.

  4. Can't make a shift? Click Offer this shift for swap. If another volunteer has given one up, it shows under “shifts other volunteers are giving up” with a Take it button.

Sign-ups and swaps still respect the screening rules — a volunteer can't take a role they're not cleared for.

Reference

Glossary

Net assets
A nonprofit's version of equity — what's left after liabilities. Reported in two classes: with and without donor restrictions.
Fund
The net-asset class a transaction belongs to. Tracked here as an analytic account you put on each income and expense line.
Purpose restriction
A donor condition that the gift be spent on a specific program or item.
Time restriction
A donor condition that the gift not be spent until a future period.
Endowment
A gift whose principal is kept permanently; only the earnings are available.
Release from restriction
Moving money from a restricted class to unrestricted once the donor's condition is met. Recorded with the release wizard.
Program
A mission activity — the pantry, the clinic, the after-school club.
Function
The Form 990 expense category: Program Services, Management & General, or Fundraising.
Analytic distribution
The field on a transaction line where you set the fund, program, and function — usually as percentages totalling 100%.
In-kind gift
A donation of goods or services rather than cash, recorded at fair value.
Quid pro quo
A payment that's part gift, part purchase (a gala ticket). Only the gift portion is deductible; the statement must say so.
LYBUNT / SYBUNT
Donors who gave Last Year / Some Year But Unfortunately Not This year — the lapsed-donor lists for reactivation appeals.
Shift
A volunteer role broken into a specific time window with its own slot count.
Grace window
How many minutes before a shift starts a volunteer is allowed to check in from their phone.
No-show
A confirmed volunteer who didn't turn up. Kept on the record, excluded from hours.

Keep this open in a tab. When something on screen doesn't match what's written here, trust the screen and tell whoever maintains the system — the menus move as the software updates.
